Tips to get the most out of /tree: 
 
\- shift+l: label checkpoints 
 - ctrl+o: cycle through to view session trees in default mode, with no-tools, user-only or labeled-only views 
\- ctrl+l: collapse to just checkpoints 

Here’s an example workflow of how to use these
